## 2. NILS design grammar (observed, mobile)

The page is described tile by tile, top to bottom. Each item gives the share of the page it occupies.

1. **Hero (~11% of the page, ≈1 viewport)**
   - A full-bleed lifestyle photograph (bed and linen, warm neutral tones).
   - The wordmark is the **only hero text**: thin high-contrast capitals, centred, in the upper third, ≈60% of the viewport wide.
   - A phone icon sits top-left and a language switch top-right.
   - **There is no navigation bar, headline or CTA in the hero.**
   - Translucent white **hand-drawn wavy lines** (4–5 strokes) cross the lower half. This is a water motif.
   - The photo **dissolves into the paper ground** through a soft gradient, so there is no hard section edge.

2. **Story**
   - Uppercase hairline display heading, 4 lines, centred, ≈34 px.
   - Centred paragraph, ≈17 px, line-height ≈1.75, ≈40 characters per line.
   - A few keywords set in bold (an SEO tactic that also helps scanning).
   - Generous space above and below (~64–100 px).

3. **Restaurant**
   - A single image inside an **arch/oval mask**, presented as a carousel.
   - **Circle outline arrow buttons** (≈39 px) overlap the lower edge of the mask, one on each side.
   - Uppercase title, a description of 2–3 lines, then an **outline pill button with a leading arrow** ("→ View details").

4. **Section ornament**
   - A small brand icon above each major heading.
   - It repeats identically and acts as a **rhythm marker**.

5. **Rooms**
   - Heading, intro and a "View all rooms" pill.
   - A **horizontal card carousel**:
     - Each card is ≈88% of the viewport wide, and **the next card peeks** at the right edge, signalling that you can swipe.
     - The image is ≈1:1 with square corners.
     - **Two action pills sit on top of the image, top-right:** Enquire (dark fill) and Book (light fill).
     - A **large mixed-case display title overlaps the image's bottom edge**, partly on the image and partly on the paper.
   - Below the image, only:
     - guest capacity ("2 guests");
     - **"FROM {price}"** in bold sans, followed by "per room incl. breakfast" in regular weight;
     - a "View details" text link.
   - Circle arrows sit bottom-right.
   - **There is no size, amenity list or spec table on the card.**

6. **Impressions (gallery teaser)**
   - A short **navy band**.
   - A widely tracked uppercase heading **overlaps two staggered photos**: the left photo is taller and starts lower; the right one is shorter and sits higher. The result is an asymmetric editorial collage.
   - A right-aligned "View all" link and centred circle arrows.

7. **Packages**
   - Back on paper, with the same ornament → heading → paragraph → pill rhythm.
   - The card grammar matches the rooms cards, adding the number of nights, a date range and a "from" price.

8. **Marquee band**
   - Navy, with 3–4 rows of very large uppercase value words running horizontally. The rows probably alternate direction.
   - It works as a **transition device** between light and dark.

9. **Social wall**
   - Filter chips (Instagram, Facebook) and a square photo feed.

10. **Events**
    - The content sits inside a **very large-radius cream container** (top corners ≈ half its width) that rises into the section.
    - A pill CTA follows.

11. **Feature bands**
    - Three or more **full-bleed photo strips stacked with no gaps**, each ≈35–40% of the viewport tall.
    - Each carries a **mixed-case display title at the bottom-left**.
    - They act as compact links to sub-pages.

12. **Footer**
    - **Ground and type:** navy, with **centred display headings** ("Address", "Contact", "Newsletter").
    - **Contact:** underlined links and circular social icons.
    - **Newsletter:** an underline-only email field with a circle-arrow submit button.
    - **Badges and links:** Booking.com and Google badges, a vouchers pill, a pipe-separated utility link list, and a muted "interesting pages" internal-link list.
    - **Awards:** award logos, with **the wavy-line motif returning in the background**, so the motif opens and closes the page.
    - **Legal:** legal links and a "powered by" credit.

13. **Persistent mobile dock**, fixed at the bottom
    - A white floating **segmented pill [Enquire (navy) | Book (light blue)]**.
    - A **circular menu (hamburger) button** to the right of the pill.
    - Two small circular shortcuts above-left (voucher, calendar) and an accessibility circle above-right.
    - **All navigation lives in the thumb zone.** Nothing at the top competes with the hero.

**System traits**
- **Only two grounds:** paper and navy. Images are the only other colour.
- **Corners:**
  - square photographs;
  - one arch mask;
  - one very-large-radius container;
  - pills and circles for controls;
  - **no small-radius cards**.
- **Mobile alignment:** a centred axis, with a ≈24 px gutter.
- **One repeated section grammar:** ornament → caps heading → centred paragraph → outline pill → carousel with circle arrows. The repetition itself is what makes the page feel cohesive.
- **Every block is readable within one viewport.** No single block requires scrolling to understand it.
- **Information density is low**, but nothing on the page is empty filler.

## 4. Desktop composition (*inferred*)

Derived from the mobile grammar and Tejjora's laptop requirement:
- The centred axis becomes **asymmetric**: copy on the left, at most ~60ch wide, with images on the right.
- Maximum content width is 1240 px. Full-bleed is used only for the hero, collages and feature bands.
- Carousels show 2.4–3 cards.
- Feature bands sit side by side (three across) instead of stacked.
- **Every composition fits a 1366×768 viewport.**

## 5. Mobile composition (observed)

- Gutter ≈24 px.
- Section gap ≈64–100 px.
- Cards ≈88vw wide, with the next card peeking.
- Body text 16–17 px, line-height ~1.75, centred.
- Headings in caps, 28–36 px, at most 4 lines.
- Hero ≈1 viewport.
- Feature bands ≈38svh.
- Fixed dock ≈60 px plus the safe area.
- Total page length ≈10 screens.

## 6. Navigation (observed, mobile)

- **No top bar.** Only a call icon (left) and the language switch (right) sit over the hero.
- The menu, the primary CTAs (Enquire/Book) and shortcuts (dates, vouchers) all live in the **bottom dock**.
- The desktop header behaviour was not observed.

## 7. Hero (observed)

- The hero is a **brand lockup over photography**, with no headline or CTA.
- A water-line overlay crosses it.
- A gradient dissolves it into the next section.
- Conversion relies on the always-visible dock rather than hero buttons.

## 8. Typography (observed)

- **Display:** thin, high-contrast hairline face. It is set in uppercase for section headings (loosely tracked; very widely tracked in short dark-band titles) and in mixed case for card, band and footer titles (large, ≈40–48 px).
- **Body:** a geometric sans, regular weight. Bold is used only for prices and keywords.
- **Buttons:** sentence case, ≈15 px, with a leading arrow glyph.
- **Price:** "FROM" in uppercase bold sans. The qualifier follows in regular weight.
- The contrast between display and body comes mainly from **size and stroke contrast**, not weight.

## 9. Photography (observed)

- Warm neutral, airy, lifestyle-led: people in the space, details, materials.
- Crops are mostly square or landscape. One arch mask. No rounded photo corners.
- Images are always **legible at the size shown**. There are no partial giant images.
- Photo pairs are staggered for an editorial rhythm.

## 10. Rooms (observed)

The card holds the minimum needed to choose, and the detail page holds the specs:
- Actions sit **on the image**.
- The title overlaps the image edge.
- Capacity plus a "from" price including what the price covers.
- One link.
- Browsing by swipe, with arrow buttons as an alternative.

## 11. Booking (observed, mobile)

- **Persistent segmented Enquire | Book pill**, plus a calendar circle shortcut.
- No date fields are visible on the page.
- Dates presumably open in a sheet or on the booking engine (not observed).
- Interaction density on the page itself is minimal.

## 12. Motion (partly inferred; the screenshot is static)

These points are implied by the components rather than seen moving:
- carousels sliding;
- the marquee scrolling;
- wave lines possibly animated;
- pills with hover fills.

The page does not appear to use scroll-jacking: each block is a normal-height section.

## 13. Scroll storytelling (observed)

Narrative comes from **sequence and ground changes**, not from long sticky runways:

> paper story → navy band → paper → navy marquee → paper → image bands → navy footer

## 14. Section transitions (observed)

These are what make the page feel like one continuous piece rather than stacked components:
1. A gradient dissolve out of the hero.
2. The repeated ornament → heading → paragraph → pill sequence.
3. **Overlaps across boundaries:** titles over image edges, headings over collage photos, arrows over the mask, a container rising into its section.
4. Only two grounds, with **short** dark bands.
5. The same motif at the start (hero waves) and the end (footer waves).
6. Gap-less image bands.

## 15. Microinteractions and details (observed)

- **Circle outline arrows:** ≈39 px, hairline border.
- **Outline pills:** with a leading "→".
- **Segmented pill:** dark and light halves.
- **Footer email field:** underline only, with a circle submit.
- **Link styles:** underlined links in the footer; bold inline keywords in body copy.
- **Footer icons:** circular, on a tinted ground.
- **Accessibility:** a floating accessibility button.
- **Not used:** card shadows (except on the floating dock), gradients other than the hero dissolve, counters or numbering.

## 16. Tejjora translation

Tejjora takes the **grammar** and expresses it with Tejjora's own identity.

| Reference grammar | Tejjora expression |
|---|---|
| Navy + paper grounds | **Lake `#022D36` + paper `#F8F5ED`**, with cream type on lake, from the logo |
| Steel-blue secondary pill | Mist `#9FBED2` as the secondary, or cream on lake |
| Anchor ornament | **A logo-derived waterline glyph with an ember dot** (the logo's fruit) |
| Hand-drawn wave strokes | **Tejjora's waterline**, redrawn from the logo's line work; used in the hero, the Waterline Day progress line and the footer |
| Oval/arch mask | **The "Awadh arch"**: a rounded-top arch referencing Lucknow's architecture. Used once or twice per page at most. |
| Hairline display caps | **Cormorant Garamond 300/400**: caps with +0.04em tracking for section headings, mixed case for titles |
| Geometric sans body | **Manrope**, which is already loaded and suits small mobile sizes |
| Wordmark-like small caps | **Cinzel**, for eyebrows only |
| Accent colour | **Ember `#FF6437` as an accent only:** the dot in the Book pill, focus rings, the active Waterline step. **It is not used as a button fill.** |
| Enquire / Book dock | **[Ask | Book]**. "Ask" opens WhatsApp or the AI. Book opens the date sheet or `/book`. |
| Packages | Offers, **only when admin has published one** |
| Social wall, events, awards | **Not adopted.** There are no verified equivalents. |

The intended feel: premium, boutique, calm, cinematic, editorial, warm, contemporary, hospitality-led, confident, interactive and original. It should read as Tejjora's lake and Awadh character, not as an Austrian lake-resort clone.

## 17. Patterns NOT to copy

- The NILS wordmark, the anchor icon, its exact wave strokes, its colours as a palette, any of its copy, photographs, awards or partner logos.
- The social media wall: Tejjora has no verified profiles.
- The events and conferences block: not a verified Tejjora offering.
- "Adults only" or similar positioning statements.
- OTA rating badges, star ratings or award badges, unless verified data exists (see the ledger).
- A newsletter form: there is no backend or consent flow. Add it only if requested.
- SEO "interesting pages" lists that point to thin or doorway pages. Link only to real pages.
- Exact pixel dimensions or layouts. Tejjora proportions come from §19.
- Circle buttons smaller than 44 px. The reference's ≈39 px is below Tejjora's accessibility rule.

## 19. Responsive rules

| Width | Gutter | Section headings | Card width | Hero | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 360 | 20 | 28 px caps, at most 4 lines | 86vw | 92svh, lockup ≤70vw | The dock fits in 360 px: menu circle 48, pill flexible, AI circle above |
| 390 | 24 | 30–32 px | 86vw | 92svh | The reference viewport |
| 430 | 24 | 34–36 px | 84vw | 92svh | |
| 768 | 32 | 40 px | 2.1 cards | 85svh | Top bar is still minimal and the dock remains |
| 1024 | 40 | 44 px | 2.4 cards | 100svh minus console | Desktop navigation appears; the dock is replaced by the header Book pill and the hero console |
| 1366×768 / 1440×900 / 1536×864 | 48–64 | clamp, with a 9svh cap | 3 cards | Lockup, one-line positioning and console visible without scrolling | Every block ≤100svh; Waterline runway ≤150svh |
| 1920+ | auto, max width 1240 | capped | 3 cards | — | Full-bleed only for hero, collage and bands |

General rules:
- Touch targets ≥44 px.
- Body text ≥16 px.
- No hover-only information.
- Content bottom padding equals the dock height plus the safe area.
